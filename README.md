Discover the world of music and news with All World Radio! A simple yet powerful app for streaming radio online.
Key Features:
•	Wide Variety: Hundreds of stations across different genres and countries.
•	Smart Theme: The app automatically switches between light and dark modes based on the time of day.
•	Favorites: Save your top stations with a single tap for quick access.
•	Sleep Timer: Fall asleep to your favorite tunes with an automatic shut-off timer.
•	Adaptive Design: Optimized interface for a great experience on both phones and tablets.
Listen to what you love, no matter where you are!
